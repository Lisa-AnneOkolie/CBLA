The Heart Behind It

Every candle is poured with intention.

Candles by Lisa-Anne is a home fragrance studio built on care — candles, diffusers, room sprays and gift sets, hand-poured in small batches and finished by hand, right down to the last detail of the packaging.

It isn't just about scent. It's about the moment a candle creates — a little more calm, a little more warmth, a little more light in the room.

"Let your light inspire hope, and your heart inspire change."
